Mora is the county seat of Kanabec County, a small community of about 3,600 with a Swedish heritage that goes back to the town's founding. The housing stock is a mix of older homes in the town center and rural properties on larger lots along the county roads. Many properties in the Mora area are on well and septic.

## What We Look for in Mora

The older in-town housing has the characteristics you'd expect in a small Minnesota county seat - construction from the early to mid-1900s with systems that have been maintained and updated to varying degrees over the decades. We check every accessible system and document the condition with photos.

Rural properties around Mora tend to sit on larger lots with more exposure to wind and weather. Additions and outbuildings are common, and the quality of that construction varies. We pay attention to how additions tie into the original structure, whether footing depths meet the 60-inch requirement, and whether electrical runs to outbuildings were done safely.
