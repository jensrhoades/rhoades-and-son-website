Pine City is the county seat of Pine County, with a population around 3,600 and a larger variety of housing stock than the smaller corridor communities. As the county hub, Pine City has more commercial and residential development, and the homes here range from older in-town properties to newer builds on the edges of the community.

## What a Home Inspection Covers in Pine City

The variety of housing eras here means we see a wider range of conditions than in the smaller towns. Older downtown homes with original systems. Mid-century ranches with updates of varying quality. Newer construction with builder-grade materials. Each era has its own inspection priorities, and we adjust accordingly.

As the county seat, Pine City has more municipal infrastructure than some of its neighbors, but plenty of properties on the outskirts are still on well and septic. Pine County requires a septic compliance inspection at point of sale for properties with septic systems.

The 60-inch frost footing requirement applies to all construction in this part of the state. Additions, garages, and decks are the most common places where footing depth comes into question.
