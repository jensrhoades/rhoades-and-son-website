Silver Bay was built around the mining operation - first Reserve Mining, now Northshore Mining. Most of the housing stock dates to the 1950s and 1960s, built during the town's rapid growth period. That means the homes here are relatively uniform in age and construction style, which is unusual for the North Shore. It also means many of these homes are now 60 to 70 years old, right at the point where major systems start needing replacement if they haven't been updated already.

## What We Look for in Silver Bay

Homes from this era often have original electrical panels, early forced-air heating systems, and plumbing materials that are approaching the end of their expected lifespan. Insulation standards were different then, and many of these homes are under-insulated by current standards - something you feel in the heating bills before you see it in the inspection, but we check it either way.

Silver Bay sits right on Lake Superior with full exposure to everything the lake puts out. Wind, moisture, and heavy snow are constants. Ice damming, roof wear, and exterior maintenance are ongoing concerns for any home here. We check ventilation, insulation, and the condition of every exterior surface we can access.
