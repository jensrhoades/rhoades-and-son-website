Aitkin is the county seat of Aitkin County, a small community of about 2,100 in a rural, forested part of central Minnesota. The housing stock is predominantly older, reflecting the town's long history as a farming and forestry community. Properties in the Aitkin area are often on larger lots with well water and septic systems.

## What We Look for in Aitkin

Older homes in Aitkin have the characteristics of small-town rural construction across the region. Systems that have been maintained and updated over the decades, with varying quality of work. Rural properties with outbuildings, additions, and owner-built improvements that need the same level of attention as the main house.

The winters here are serious. Aitkin is far enough from Lake Superior to lose any moisture moderation, but the cold is real. Heating systems work hard, and insulation matters. We evaluate both, along with every other accessible system, and document everything with photos.
