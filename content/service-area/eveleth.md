Eveleth has the oldest housing stock in our entire service area. The median construction year is 1938, and more than 53% of the homes here were built before 1940. This is mining-era construction from when Eveleth was growing alongside the iron mines. The homes are small by modern standards, built for working families, and they've been standing through Iron Range winters for 85 years or more.

## What a Home Inspection Means in a Town This Old

When more than half the housing stock is 85-plus years old, the inspection question isn't whether there are issues. It's how well the previous owners addressed them. Every system in these homes - electrical, plumbing, heating, foundation - has been through multiple lifecycle replacements by now. The quality of that work varies enormously from house to house.

We see homes where the wiring was professionally updated to modern standards and others where someone spliced new wire onto old knob-and-tube in the attic and called it done. We see furnaces that were replaced properly and others sitting on bases that aren't level. The inspection documents what's there, how it was done, and whether it's safe.

Foundations in Eveleth are often the original stone or block from the 1920s and 1930s. These can last indefinitely if they've been kept dry, but water intrusion over decades will deteriorate mortar joints and blocks. We check for cracking patterns, moisture evidence, and signs of structural movement.
