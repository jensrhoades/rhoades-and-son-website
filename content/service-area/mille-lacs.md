The communities around Mille Lacs Lake - Garrison, Isle, Wahkon, Onamia, and the surrounding area - share a common housing pattern. A large number of properties here were built as seasonal or resort homes, and many are now being sold to buyers looking for year-round lake living.

## Seasonal to Permanent Home Inspection

This is the same pattern we see across the Brainerd Lakes area, but Mille Lacs has an even higher concentration of seasonal properties. A home that was used for fishing weekends and a few weeks in the summer was built to a different standard than a year-round residence. The foundation, insulation, heating system, and plumbing all need to be evaluated through that lens.

We check whether the structure has been upgraded for year-round use or whether it's still essentially a seasonal cabin with the heat left on. The difference matters, and the inspection report makes it clear.
