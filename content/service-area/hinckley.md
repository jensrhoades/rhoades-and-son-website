Hinckley has about 1,900 residents and sits at the crossroads of I-35 and Highway 48. The town has a mix of older homes in the original community and some newer development. Housing is affordable, and the mix of construction eras means each inspection has its own priorities.

## What We Look for in Hinckley

Older homes in Hinckley share the same small-town construction patterns found across Pine County. We evaluate electrical, plumbing, heating, and structural systems based on what we can see and access, and document everything with photos and locations.

Newer homes have different priorities - grading and drainage, builder-grade materials and their expected lifespans, and whether the construction met code at the time it was built. Neither older nor newer means better or worse. It just means different things to check.

Pine County requires a septic compliance inspection at point of sale for properties on septic systems. That's handled separately from the [home inspection](/) but is part of the overall transaction.
