North Branch is a growing exurban community in Chisago County with a population around 10,800. Like Cambridge, it draws buyers who commute to the metro but want more space and lower housing costs. The result is a housing market with a wide range of construction eras - older rural properties on acreage, established in-town neighborhoods, and new development going up on the edges.

## What a Home Inspection Covers in North Branch

The newer construction in North Branch makes up a significant share of the available inventory. We inspect homes in all stages of their lifecycle, and newer homes have their own priorities. Grading and drainage, material quality, HVAC sizing for the floor plan, and whether finishes were installed properly - these are the things that show up in newer construction.

Older rural properties in the North Branch area often sit on larger lots with well water and septic systems. Outbuildings, additions, and owner-built improvements are common. We give these the same close attention as any other part of the inspection.

## Chisago County Requirements

Chisago County requires a septic compliance inspection at point of sale for properties on septic systems. Existing compliant certificates are valid for three years, and new installations are valid for five. That's a separate process from the [home inspection](/), but it's part of the overall transaction and something to account for in your timeline.
