Proctor is a small community just west of Duluth with roots as a railroad town. The housing stock reflects that history - a lot of older worker housing from the early and mid-1900s, compact floor plans, and construction that was functional for its era. These homes have held up, but a century of use and northern Minnesota weather means every system has been through multiple lifecycles.

## What We Look for in Proctor

Older homes in Proctor share the same patterns we see across the Duluth area: original wiring that may have been partially updated, older plumbing materials, and foundations that have been through decades of freeze-thaw. The compact lot sizes in town mean homes are close together, and drainage between properties matters. Water that doesn't have anywhere to go ends up against foundations.

The 60-inch frost footing requirement applies here. Garages and additions on older lots are common places where the original construction may not have met current depth requirements.
