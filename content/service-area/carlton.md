Carlton is a small community in Carlton County with a mix of in-town housing and rural properties on larger lots. The housing stock ranges from older homes in the original town center to newer construction on the outskirts. Many properties in the Carlton area are on private well and septic systems rather than municipal utilities.

## What We Look for in Carlton

Rural properties bring their own set of inspection considerations. Homes on well water have different plumbing dynamics than municipal-connected homes. Outbuildings, detached garages, and additions are common on rural lots. We pay attention to how these connect to the main structure and whether the work was done to the same standard. The 60-inch frost footing requirement applies to every structure, not just the main house.

Older homes in the Carlton area share the characteristics of small-town construction across the region - systems that have been updated at various points, with varying quality of work. We evaluate what's there now and document the condition with photos and specific locations.
