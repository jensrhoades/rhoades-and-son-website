Meet Dusty Rhoades

Most inspectors can tell you something is wrong. Dusty can tell you why. He spent twenty years in residential construction, framing walls, running mechanicals, roofing, finishing. He knows what's behind the drywall because he's the one who put it there.

He also teaches construction to high school students, which means he's spent years breaking down building concepts for people who've never picked up a hammer. Your report won't be full of jargon. Your walkthrough won't leave you guessing.

Two generations, one standard

Rhoades & Son is Dusty and his son Gunnar. When Dusty decided to put his construction background to work as an inspector, bringing Gunnar along was a natural fit. Same attention to detail, same commitment to doing the job right.

Every inspection follows InterNACHI's Standards of Practice. Every accessible system gets checked, from the roof to the foundation. And every report gets a walkthrough so you know exactly what you're looking at.
