Sandstone is a small Pine County community on I-35 with about 2,800 residents. Named for the sandstone quarries that once drove the local economy, the town has a mix of older in-town homes and rural properties on the surrounding land. Housing here is affordable, and that draws buyers who are looking for value - which is exactly when knowing the true condition of a property matters most.

## What We Look for in Sandstone

The housing stock here is a mix of eras. Older homes in town share the characteristics of small-town construction across the region: systems that have been updated at various points over the decades, with varying quality of work. Rural properties add the considerations that come with well water, septic systems, and structures that are more exposed to the elements.

We check every accessible system, document findings with photos, and deliver the report within 24 hours. Pine County requires a septic compliance inspection at point of sale for properties on septic - that's a separate process, but something to plan for in your transaction.
