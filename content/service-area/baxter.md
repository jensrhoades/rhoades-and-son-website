Baxter is the commercial and growth hub adjacent to Brainerd, with a population around 8,500 and more newer construction than its neighbor. While Brainerd's median home dates to 1971, Baxter's development has been more recent, driven by retail growth and new residential neighborhoods.

## What a Home Inspection Covers in Baxter

Newer construction dominates the Baxter market, and newer homes have their own inspection priorities. Grading and drainage around recently built homes sometimes settles in ways that direct water toward the foundation rather than away from it. Builder-grade materials - siding, roofing, fixtures - have predictable lifespans that are often shorter than homeowners expect. HVAC systems sized too small or too large for the floor plan show up in utility bills before they show up anywhere else, but we note the equipment and its capacity.

Homes in the 10- to 20-year-old range are hitting the point where the first round of maintenance items comes due. Caulking, weatherstripping, water heater replacement, exterior paint - none of these are emergencies, but knowing where a home sits in its maintenance cycle helps you plan.

The Brainerd Lakes area sandy soils and the 60-inch frost footing requirement apply here the same as everywhere else in the region.
