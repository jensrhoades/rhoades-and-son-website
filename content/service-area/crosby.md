Crosby is a Cuyuna Range mining town with about 2,400 residents. The housing stock here has more in common with the Iron Range mining towns than with neighboring Brainerd - older construction from the mining era, compact floor plans, and homes that have been standing through Minnesota winters for generations.

## What We Look for in Crosby

The old mining-town housing in Crosby shares the same characteristics we see across the Iron Range: pre-war and mid-century construction with systems that have been through multiple rounds of updates. Electrical, plumbing, heating, and foundations all get evaluated based on their current condition, not their original vintage.

The Cuyuna Range mine pits are now recreational lakes, and some properties near the old mining areas may have unique ground conditions. This is background context we carry when inspecting homes in the area.

Older homes are where our background matters most. Dusty spent 20 years in residential construction, building houses from the ground up across northern Minnesota. When we open a panel or look at a foundation, we're comparing what we see to what we've built with our own hands.
