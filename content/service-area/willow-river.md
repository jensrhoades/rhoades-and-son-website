Willow River is a small, rural community in Pine County with a population around 300. The housing here is almost entirely single-family homes on larger lots, many of them on private well and septic. Construction eras range from pre-war farmhouses to mid-century homes to newer builds scattered along the county roads.

## Rural Properties Need a Careful Home Inspection

Rural homes in and around Willow River often come with features that add complexity to an inspection. Additions built over the years, sometimes with permits and sometimes without. Outbuildings and garages with their own electrical runs. Heating systems working harder because the house is more exposed to wind than an in-town property. Longer driveways and grading that affect how water moves around the foundation.

We inspect rural properties regularly and know what to look for. The 60-inch frost footing requirement doesn't change just because a property is on a back road, and neither do the standards for electrical, plumbing, and structural integrity.

Pine County requires a septic compliance inspection at point of sale for properties on septic systems. That's a separate process from the home inspection, but it's something to plan for in your transaction timeline and budget.
