Pequot Lakes is a lake resort community in Crow Wing County with about 2,300 year-round residents and a significant seasonal population. The housing here is a mix of newer construction, older lake cabins, and everything in between. A high percentage of properties in the Pequot Lakes area were built as seasonal homes and have either been converted to year-round use or are in the process of being sold to buyers who plan to live there permanently.

## Seasonal Conversions Are the Story Here

The same pattern we see across the Brainerd Lakes area applies in Pequot Lakes, maybe even more so. Cabins built for summer use are being marketed as permanent homes. We focus on whether the building envelope, insulation, heating, and plumbing are adequate for year-round Minnesota habitation. A home that's comfortable in July may not be livable in January without significant upgrades.

Properties near the lakes also deal with high water tables, sandy soils, and moisture conditions that affect basements and lower-level spaces. We check for water intrusion evidence and note the grading around the foundation.
