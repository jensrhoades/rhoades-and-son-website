Minnesota weather and your home

Minnesota has two frost zones. Zone 1, which is everything north of the metro, requires 60-inch frost footings. Zone 2 in the Twin Cities and south requires 42-inch. We check additions, attached garages, and decks to make sure footings went deep enough. If they didn't, freeze-thaw will eventually let you know, and it won't be subtle.

In winter, we can't always see shingles or flashing under two feet of snow. But the snow tells us a lot. Bare spots mean heat loss. Even snow means the insulation is doing its job. Ice dams on the eaves mean the ventilation isn't. We note what we can and can't evaluate, and we're clear about it in the report.
