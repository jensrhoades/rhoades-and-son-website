Cambridge is the southern end of our service area and the closest community to the metro. With a population around 9,300 and steady growth, the housing market here is different from the northern and central communities. There's more new construction, higher home values, and a faster pace of transactions driven by buyers who commute to the Twin Cities.

## What a Home Inspection Covers in Cambridge

The mix of housing eras here is wider than in the smaller corridor towns. Cambridge has older homes in the original downtown, mid-century neighborhoods, and significant newer development on the growing edges of town. Each era brings different inspection priorities.

Newer construction is a bigger share of the market here than in most of our service area. We inspect new and newer homes regularly, and "new" doesn't mean "no findings." Grading that settles toward the foundation, builder-grade materials with short lifespans, and construction that was done to code but done fast are all things we see. The inspection catches them before you've been living with the problem for a year.

Older homes in Cambridge share the same characteristics as small-town housing across the region, with systems that have been through multiple rounds of updates. The quality of that work varies, and the inspection documents what's actually there.

## Cambridge's Market

Higher home values in Cambridge mean more is at stake in every transaction. An inspection that catches a foundation issue before closing is a different conversation when the home is listed at $350,000 versus $130,000 on the Range. The stakes are proportional, and so is the value of knowing what you're buying.
