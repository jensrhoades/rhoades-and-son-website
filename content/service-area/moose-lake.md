Moose Lake sits right along I-35 in Carlton County, with a mix of older in-town homes and rural properties on the surrounding land. The town has about 2,800 people and the kind of housing stock you find up and down this stretch of the corridor - homes from a few different eras, some maintained carefully, some less so.

## What We Look for in Moose Lake

The older downtown housing shares the same general characteristics as small-town homes across the region: construction from the early to mid-1900s with systems that have been through multiple rounds of updates. We evaluate what's there now, not what was there originally, and document the condition of every accessible system.

Properties outside of town are often on larger lots with well water and septic systems. These properties tend to have outbuildings, additions, and owner-built improvements that get a close look during the inspection. The 60-inch frost footing requirement applies to all structures, and additions and detached garages are common places where it gets missed.
